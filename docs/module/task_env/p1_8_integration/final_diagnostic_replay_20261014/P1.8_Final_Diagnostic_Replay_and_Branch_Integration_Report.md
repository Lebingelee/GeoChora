## Contact evidence and limits

GeoPhys contact diagnostics are body-aggregate, sampled once per outer boundary; pair-level identities are unavailable. Thus 50/100 Hz traces are sparse relative to native 500 Hz physics, and their reported contact impulses cannot be directly equated to MuJoCo native contact forces. In MuJoCo D1, sampled Nut/table contact involves `SquareNut_collision_0..4` and the table geom; target contacts are sampled every 10 control boundaries plus the final boundary. Those observations support a contact/settling difference early in the trajectory, but they do not quantify a matched pair-by-pair force history. No friction, collision geometry, solver, mass/inertia or controller intervention was performed in this campaign.
